## Getting Started

Welcome to Stapelwerk! Our AI-powered platform helps you discover, build, and optimize technology stacks tailored to your specific needs. Whether you're starting a new project, exploring new technologies, or looking to optimize your existing stack, our intelligent recommendations will guide you to the best choices.

### Creating Your Account

1. **Sign Up**: Visit [stapelwerk.com](https://stapelwerk.com) and click "Get Started"
2. **Choose Your Sign-Up Method**:
   - Email and password
   - Google OAuth
   - GitHub OAuth (recommended for developers)
3. **Complete Your Profile**: Tell us about your experience level and interests

### Your First Stack

Once you're signed in, you'll be guided through creating your first technology stack:

1. **Project Type**: Select what you're building (Web App, Mobile App, API, etc.)
2. **Experience Level**: Choose your technical expertise level
3. **Requirements**: Specify your project needs and constraints
4. **Get Recommendations**: Our AI will suggest the best technologies for your stack

### Dashboard Overview

Your personal dashboard provides:
- **Recent Stacks**: Quick access to your current projects
- **Recommended Technologies**: Personalized suggestions based on your activity
- **Popular Templates**: Trending stack templates from the community
- **Learning Resources**: Tutorials and guides for your selected technologies

## AI-Powered Recommendations

Our AI recommendation engine is the heart of Stapelwerk, providing intelligent suggestions based on your preferences, project requirements, and community insights.

### How AI Recommendations Work

#### Personalization Engine
Our system learns from your behavior to provide increasingly accurate recommendations:

- **Stack History**: Analyzes your previous technology choices
- **Interaction Patterns**: Tracks what you click, explore, and implement
- **Feedback Learning**: Improves based on your ratings and feedback
- **Context Awareness**: Considers your current project context

#### Recommendation Types

##### 1. Technology Recommendations
**Purpose**: Suggests specific technologies for your stack components

**How to Use**:
1. Navigate to "Stack Builder" or create a new stack
2. Select a category (Frontend, Backend, Database, etc.)
3. View AI-powered recommendations with reasoning
4. Click on any technology to see detailed information

**Example**:
```
📱 Frontend Framework Recommendations

🔥 React (Confidence: 95%)
   Reason: Perfect match for your JavaScript preference and 
           component-based architecture needs. Excellent 
           community support for your team size.

⭐ Vue.js (Confidence: 87%)
   Reason: Great alternative with gentler learning curve. 
           Strong ecosystem for rapid development.

💡 Angular (Confidence: 72%)
   Reason: Enterprise-grade solution. Consider if you 
           prefer TypeScript-first development.
```

##### 2. Stack Template Recommendations
**Purpose**: Suggests complete, pre-built technology stacks

**Features**:
- Curated by AI and community experts
- Tested combinations that work well together
- Includes setup instructions and best practices
- Regular updates with latest versions

##### 3. Complementary Technology Suggestions
**Purpose**: Recommends additional tools that work well with your current selections

**Example**:
- Added React? We'll suggest compatible state management, testing, and build tools
- Chose PostgreSQL? We'll recommend ORMs, connection pooling, and backup solutions

### Understanding Recommendation Scores

Each recommendation includes a confidence score and explanation:

- **90-100%**: Excellent fit, highly recommended
- **75-89%**: Good choice, worth considering
- **60-74%**: Viable option with trade-offs
- **Below 60%**: May not be ideal for your requirements

##### Deployment (Direct Apply)
- Use the Deploy button in Stack Builder to open the Direct Apply modal.
- Pick a Deployment Target (create one inline if needed) and optionally a saved Stack.
- Click Create & Start Apply to run an apply job and watch live status/logs.
- Prefer CI? Generate a CI YAML snippet instead of running server-side apply.
- See the full guide: docs/deployment/direct-apply-ui.md

Example CI snippet (rendered for your Target):

```yaml path=null start=null
stages: [deploy]

apply:
  stage: deploy
  image: bitnami/kubectl:latest
  script:
    - kubectl config use-context "$KUBE_CONTEXT"
    - kubectl apply -f k8s/deploy.yaml
  rules:
    - if: $CI_COMMIT_BRANCH == "main"
```

### API Integration

#### REST API Access
**Programmatic access to Stapelwerk features**:

```javascript
// Get personalized recommendations
const recommendations = await fetch('/api/recommendations', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: { 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    projectType: 'web-app',
    experienceLevel: 'intermediate',
    preferences: {
      frontend: ['react', 'vue'],
      backend: ['node', 'python']
    }
  })
});

// Create a new stack
const stack = await fetch('/api/stacks', {
  method: 'POST',
  headers: { 'Content-Type': 'application/json' },
  body: JSON.stringify({
    name: 'My Web App',
    technologies: ['react', 'node', 'postgresql']
  })
});
```

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues and Solutions

#### Recommendation Issues

##### "Not Getting Relevant Recommendations"
**Possible Causes and Solutions**:

1. **Incomplete Profile**
   - Solution: Update your experience level and technology preferences
   - Go to Profile Settings → Preferences
   - Add your favorite languages and frameworks

2. **Limited Activity History**
   - Solution: Interact more with the platform
   - Browse templates, rate technologies, create stacks
   - The AI learns from your behavior patterns

3. **Outdated Preferences**
   - Solution: Review and update your preferences regularly
   - Technology interests change over time
   - Update your profile to reflect current interests

##### "Recommendations Are Too Basic/Advanced"
**Possible Causes and Solutions**:

1. **Incorrect Experience Level**
   - Solution: Adjust your experience level in profile settings
   - Be honest about your actual skill level
   - Consider different levels for different technology areas

2. **Mixed Signals in Behavior**
   - Solution: Provide consistent feedback on recommendations
   - Rate technologies based on appropriateness for your level
   - Use the "Not interested" option for irrelevant suggestions

#### Stack Building Issues

##### "Can't Find Specific Technology"
**Possible Causes and Solutions**:

1. **Technology Not in Database**
   - Solution: Request new technology addition
   - Use the "Suggest Technology" feature
   - Provide information about the technology's importance

2. **Incorrect Search Terms**
   - Solution: Try alternative names or categories
   - Use broader category searches
   - Check for common abbreviations or alternative names

##### "Stack Compatibility Warnings"
**Understanding and Resolving Conflicts**:

1. **Version Conflicts**
   - Solution: Update to compatible versions
   - Check our compatibility database
   - Consider alternative technologies if conflicts persist

2. **Architecture Mismatches**
   - Solution: Review architectural decisions
   - Consider different deployment patterns
   - Consult community discussions for similar challenges

#### Performance Issues

##### "Slow Loading Times"
**Troubleshooting Steps**:

1. **Check Internet Connection**
   - Try refreshing the page
   - Check other websites for connectivity issues

2. **Clear Browser Cache**
   - Clear browser cache and cookies
   - Try incognito/private browsing mode

3. **Disable Browser Extensions**
   - Temporarily disable ad blockers and extensions
   - Some extensions may interfere with our AI features

## FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: How does the AI recommendation system work?**
A: Our AI uses machine learning algorithms including collaborative filtering and content-based filtering to analyze your preferences, project requirements, and community data to suggest the most suitable technologies for your needs.

**Q: Is Stapelwerk free to use?**
A: Yes, Stapelwerk offers a free tier with access to basic recommendations and templates. Premium features include advanced analytics, priority support, and unlimited private stacks.

**Q: Can I use Stapelwerk for team projects?**
A: Absolutely! Stapelwerk supports team collaboration with shared stacks, role-based permissions, and team analytics.

### Technical Questions

**Q: How often are technology recommendations updated?**
A: Our database is updated weekly with new technologies, version updates, and community feedback. The AI models are retrained monthly to incorporate new data.

**Q: Can I contribute my own templates?**
A: Yes! We encourage community contributions. You can submit templates through the "Share Template" feature, and they'll go through a community review process.

**Q: How do you ensure recommendation accuracy?**
A: We combine multiple data sources: community feedback, technology documentation, compatibility testing, and continuous learning from user interactions.

### Privacy and Security

**Q: How is my data protected?**
A: We use industry-standard encryption and security practices. Your data is never sold to third parties, and we provide full transparency about data usage.

**Q: Can I delete my account and data?**
A: Yes, you can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time through your account settings.

**Q: Do you track my activity outside of Stapelwerk?**
A: No, we only track your activity within the Stapelwerk platform to improve recommendations and user experience.

### Integration Questions

**Q: Is there an API for Stapelwerk?**
A: Yes, we offer a REST API for developers to integrate Stapelwerk features into their own tools and workflows.

**Q: Are there IDE plugins available?**
A: We currently offer plugins for VS Code and JetBrains IDEs, with more integrations planned.

**Q: Can I export my stack configurations?**
A: Yes, you can export your stacks in various formats including JSON, YAML, and as documentation files.
